Why new, demo and used matter

It is the first question Revvy asks, because it changes what applies:

  • Luxury car tax is charged on the first retail sale. Buying used avoids it entirely — a genuine saving on an expensive car.
  • Dealer delivery and factory options are first-sale charges. A used car's advertised price already contains them, so the fields disappear.
  • Guaranteed Future Value is a manufacturer program tied to a new or demonstrator vehicle. There is no factory-backed guarantee to offer on a second-hand car, so the option is removed rather than quietly producing a number nobody could be offered.
  • Depreciation is the big one. The steep first-year drop belongs to the first owner. A used car has already taken it, so only the ongoing rate applies from here — which is why used cars climb out of negative equity sooner.
  • The electric vehicle FBT exemption only reaches cars first held and used on or after 1 July 2022. A cheap older used EV does not qualify, however far under the threshold it sits.

Drive-away or before on-roads?

Advertised prices are quoted both ways, and confusing them is worth thousands. Tell Revvy which one you have entered. For a drive-away price it works backwards to separate the vehicle from the duty and registration inside it — stamp duty depends on the vehicle's value, so this cannot be done by simple subtraction, and getting it right keeps the luxury car tax and the FBT base value correct.

Plain-English glossary

Balloon payment (residual)

A lump sum left at the end of the loan. It lowers your regular repayment because you are paying off less of the car each month — but you pay interest on that deferred amount for the whole term, so the loan costs more overall. At the end you pay it out, refinance it, or sell the car. If the car is worth less than the balloon, you are in negative equity.

Guaranteed Future Value (GFV)

Sold under names like Toyota Access, Mazda Choice and Volkswagen Choice. It is a balloon payment that the manufacturer guarantees to honour as a trade-in value, provided you stay inside the kilometre allowance and condition standards. At the end you can trade it in and walk away, pay the GFV and keep the car, or refinance. Exceed the kilometre allowance and you pay an excess charge per kilometre.

Novated lease

A three-way arrangement between you, your employer and a financier. Your employer deducts the lease and running costs from your salary — mostly before tax — which lowers your taxable income. The financier claims the GST on the purchase price, so you finance a GST-exclusive amount. Because you get a car for private use, fringe benefits tax applies.

FBT and the Employee Contribution Method (ECM)

Under the statutory formula the taxable value of the car benefit is 20% of the car's base value each year. ECM cancels that out: you contribute the same amount from post-tax salary, reducing the FBT taxable value to nil. That is why a novated lease quote shows both a pre-tax and a post-tax deduction.

The electric vehicle FBT exemption

Eligible battery electric vehicles first held and used from 1 July 2022, priced under the fuel-efficient luxury car tax threshold, are exempt from FBT — so no ECM is needed and the whole package can be pre-tax. Plug-in hybrids stopped being eligible from 1 April 2025 unless a binding commitment was already in place. Exempt does not mean invisible: the benefit is still included in your reportable fringe benefits amount, which counts towards study loan repayments, the Medicare levy surcharge and various income tests.

Chattel mortgage

A business purchase where you own the vehicle from the outset and the lender takes a mortgage over it. A GST-registered business can generally claim the GST credit up front (capped at one-eleventh of the car limit), and claim interest and depreciation as deductions to the extent of business use.

Comparison rate vs effective rate

A comparison rate is a regulated figure lenders must publish, calculated on a standard example ($30,000 over 5 years for car loans). Revvy shows an effective rate instead: the rate implied by your actual cash flows, including establishment and monthly fees. It answers the question the comparison rate is meant to answer, but for your loan.

Luxury car tax (LCT)

A 33% tax on the value of a car above the LCT threshold, excluding the GST component. Fuel-efficient vehicles get a higher threshold. LCT is paid on the way in and is not refundable, and it forms part of the car's base value for FBT.

Manufacturers sometimes advertise that they will pay the LCT for you. Strictly they cannot: the tax is levied on the supplier and remitted to the ATO, and there is no mechanism to waive it. What such a campaign actually does is discount the vehicle by around the same amount, which comes to the same thing for you as the buyer. Revvy has a toggle to model this — but check that the drive-away price really has moved by the full amount, rather than the discount quietly replacing one you would have negotiated anyway.

Stamp duty

Motor vehicle duty is a state tax, so it changes at the border. Revvy models the published scales for each state and territory, including the electric vehicle concessions in the ACT and Queensland's cylinder-based rates. It is an estimate — check with your state revenue office, and use the override field if you have a real quote.

Payments in advance vs arrears

Leases are usually paid in advance (at the start of each period) and loans in arrears (at the end). Paying in advance slightly reduces the interest charged, because your payment lands before the interest accrues.

How Revvy calculates

  • Interest is accrued per payment period at the annual rate divided by the number of periods in a year. Real lenders usually accrue daily, so expect small differences.
  • When a rate change is applied, the repayment is recalculated over the remaining term — the way a variable-rate lender would.
  • Resale value uses a declining-balance depreciation curve with a separate first-year drop. Actual values depend on make, model, condition and the used market.
  • Tax uses the resident rates for the selected year, plus the Medicare levy, the levy surcharge and study loan repayments where applicable. It ignores offsets, deductions and anything else specific to you.
  • Novated lease figures are indicative. Real quotes include the packager's own fees, budgeting assumptions and insurance pricing.

Where the numbers come from

Tax rates and thresholds, the luxury car tax thresholds, the car limit, FBT rates and the ATO minimum residual scale are all editable in the Assumptions panel. They are indexed or legislated and change regularly, so verify anything you are relying on against ato.gov.au and your state revenue office.
